INCLUSION

What is Financial Inclusion?

Access to safe, official financial tools (bank accounts, mobile wallets, fair credit) so you are never held hostage by unregulated cash lenders.

DIGITAL PAYMENTS

Raast & Instant Payments

Pakistan's zero-fee State Bank system. Link your mobile number to get payments directly without needing to remember a 24-digit IBAN.

SHARIAH

Islamic Banking vs Loans

Islamic finance trades real assets and shares risk instead of charging rent on money (Riba). Takaful provides community-pooled mutual protection.

BUFFER

Emergency Fund Essentials

Save 1 to 3 months of basic living costs. Storing it in an Asaan account protects you from taking loans when medical or vehicle emergencies hit.

Financial Safety & Scam Buster

Spot the scam before it drains your wallet. Test your fraud awareness below:

🚨 INCOMING SMS TEST
"Mubarak ho! Aapka BISP 8171 program mein Rs. 50,000 cash grant pass ho gaya hai. Abhi foran apne phone per aya hua 6-digit OTP code is number per SMS karein."
RULE #1

Never Share Your OTP

No legitimate bank or wallet agent will ever ask for the SMS code sent to your phone. The OTP exists to keep them out.

RULE #2

Urgency is the Red Flag

Scammers rush you with "Account blocking in 10 minutes". Real banks send formal notices and never rush you.

RULE #3

Verify Sender IDs

Official messages from BISP come ONLY from 8171. Official bank alerts show the bank's registered alphabetic name, not an unknown 11-digit SIM.
